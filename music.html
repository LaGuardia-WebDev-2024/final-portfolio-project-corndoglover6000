this is my favorite album of all time, and i think it will be forever. an experimental indie-rock-folksy double LP where each track is better than the last? yes please! this album also has my favorite song of all time, Blue Lightning. my other favorite tracks are Spud Infinity and Little Things (if i kept going we would be here all day.) the only thing that could make it better was if it had 27 songs instead of 20. spud infinity/10

this album was my introduction to bassvictim and it's their best in my opinion. it's a really great experience to just sit down and listen to it all the way through. my favorite tracks are It's me Maria, 27a Pitfield St, and Grass is Greener. 10/10

the latest and one of the greatest big thief albums. they have a different sound but boy does it sound so good. me and my dad both loved the laraaji feature on grandmother. i was really lucky to see the whole band (including laraaji and the backup singers and musicians) play the entire album all the way through at forest hills in the fall. one of my most beautiful concert experiences. my favorite tracks are Los Angeles and Double Infinity. two infinities/10
